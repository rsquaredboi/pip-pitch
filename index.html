C&B × Locosafe · draft pitch · 16 Sep 2026

AirTag. But better. And nobody's built the brand.

The consumer GPS tracker category has real demand, mandatory subscriptions worth ~$200 a year per unit, and zero brand. Every winner on Amazon is a black plastic spy box. None of them run Meta. The attack is the Nancy playbook: take a category people are slightly embarrassed to shop for, make it beautiful and obvious, and own the channel that creates the want.

pip is a working name. Hardware, firmware and platform are Locosafe/LocoNav's Orb (750k+ vehicles tracked). C&B brings brand, creative and the Meta demand engine.

02 · Why AirTag can't win this

AirTag tells you where it was. pip tells you where it is.

AirTag is a lost-keys product that people mis-buy as a car tracker. Every one of its design choices is a gift to a real GPS brand.

AirTag (incl. 2nd gen, Jan 2026)pip (Locosafe Orb hardware)
Location sourceNo GPS, no cellular. Borrows nearby iPhones.Own GPS + 4G LTE Cat-1, 2G fallback
Live trackingSnapshots, minutes to hours stale in low-traffic areasUpdates down to seconds, one flat rate
Route historyNoneFull trip history
Geofence / speed alertsNoneYes, plus motion alerts
DiscretionAny iPhone nearby gets "AirTag Found Moving With You". Thieves check.Silent. No pairing, no chirp.
Battery~1 yr coin cell10,000 mAh, 2+ yrs standby
AndroidSetup requires AppleiOS, Android, web

AirTag is the best thing that ever happened to this category: Apple spent billions teaching 100M people that "a small thing that finds your stuff" is normal. We sell the upgrade to the people who bought the wrong one.

03 · The C&B move

Same playbook as Nancy. Different embarrassment.

Sex toys were a $15 category of purple plastic sold by intent until someone made it cute, gave it a name, and bought the want on Meta. GPS trackers are the same shape: functional, slightly shameful, spy-coded, bought only when something bad already happened. Perception shift is the product.

FROM · SPY GADGET

Suspicion

Black box, green Amazon banner, "covert", "hide from thieves", cheating-spouse energy. People buy it and hide the box.

TO · CARE OBJECT

Peace of mind

Five colourways, a lowercase name, a card you'd leave on the hall table. "know where it is." Bought for the car, the kid's first car, the dog, mum.

MOAT

Brand + sub + app

Hardware is a commodity (Shenzhen, 100-unit MOQs). The defensible layer is the name people ask for, the subscriber base, and the family app. Life360 proved the model at 3.4M subs.

WEDGE 1

Teen driver

Sept–Oct licence season, Christmas gifting. The most meme-able angle ("she thinks it's a phone charger"). Parents are already on Meta.

WEDGE 2

Car theft

Kia/Hyundai wave, keyless relay theft. "Thieves check for AirTags now." Insurance deductible does the price anchoring for us.

WEDGE 3

Aging parent

Dementia wandering, elderly drivers. Highest LTV, lowest churn, most emotional creative. Tractive did this for dogs; nobody has done it for mums.

Later: pet pod, kid pod, "Instinct"-style AI agents on the device (Prateek's add-on idea) as a premium tier. Same brand, same app, four SKUs.

04 · Hardware partner

Locosafe already cracked the hard part.

The Orb is a 90×64×23 mm, 128 g magnetic tracker with a 10,000 mAh battery, 4G LTE Cat-1 with 2G fallback, GPS/LBS, and the LocoNav platform behind it: 750k+ vehicles tracked, 40+ countries, ISO/IEC 27001, 4.5★ across 21K Google Play reviews, enterprise clients incl. Halliburton, Unilever, PepsiCo. LocoNav raised $47M (Sequoia India, Anthemis, Quiet Capital).

Today they sell it consumer-direct at $35–45 device + $19.99/mo or $159.99/yr, and B2B. What they don't have is a brand, a creative engine, or a Meta account that has spent $2.9M a month profitably.

Prateek: "Firmware + software tested with 6 lakh vehicles. Know this team, am a small angel. Can be blown up online."

07 · The attack, first 90 days

Prove it on Meta before anyone tools anything.

Weeks 1–2 · Rebrand the Orb

Name, wordmark, five colourways (silicone sleeve or paint, no new tooling), cream box. Ship the existing Locosafe app under a pip skin; full app later.

Weeks 1–3 · Funnel

Shopify + Skio subscription. Three LPs: teen driver, car theft, aging parent. One advertorial ("The AirTag Problem", Regulate Today format). One quiz ("what should you put a pip on?").

Weeks 2–4 · Creative at volume

These 100 statics are day one. Add 30 UGC/AI talking-heads (Higgsfield Soul + Kling), 10 Reddit/iMessage memes, 3 comparison carousels. Test 50 hooks in week one, the Nancy way.

Weeks 3–8 · $50k Meta test

Kill line: 30-day blended CAC > $110 or 60-day sub retention < 85%. Pass line: CAC ≤ $75 and annual-plan take ≥ 50%. Measure on Shopify orders, not pixel (learned the hard way on Nancy).

Weeks 8–12 · Decide & tool

If pass: commit a 20k-unit order, pod form factor for pet/kid/parent, own app build starts, hire a GM. If fail: we've spent $50k and 100 assets to learn a category. Cheap.

Always · Retention

Annual default at checkout, 2-pack and family bundles, "gift a pip" flow, post-purchase survey, cancel-save offers. Every incumbent review says "couldn't cancel"; we make it one tap and win on trust.
